Launching our movie clips channel

November 4, 2026

Box Office Clips is the new warm-up channel for movie clips and trailers. It is on the site now, marked warming up, and it is live in a movie campaign.

The first Short is up. There is no subscriber goal and no view target in this note. The public channel page is the record, and the card on the portfolio shows the live count.

What the channel is for

Long movie and trailer footage gets cut into Shorts: one scene, one hook, captions on the action. Brands that want that format can start on a channel that is still early, instead of waiting for a large subscriber count.
