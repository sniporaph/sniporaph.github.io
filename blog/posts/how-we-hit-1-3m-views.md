Across client channels and warm-up channels, Shorts have passed 1.3M views. That is a combined public view total, not one lucky upload.

## What the cuts had in common

The first frame is the action. The first caption line says what is happening. The clip is short enough that the hook and the payoff fit together.

Channels that are still launching, with no Shorts yet, are not part of that total. A channel only counts once it has a published view total.
