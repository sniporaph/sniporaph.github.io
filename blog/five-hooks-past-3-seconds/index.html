5 hooks that keep viewers past 3 seconds

October 28, 2026

The first three seconds decide if a Short gets a watch. These are the openings we reach for when a long video has a clear moment in it.

Start on the result

Show the win, the miss, or the reaction before you explain how it happened. The context can come in the caption.

Name the stakes in one line

"13,000 HP" or "one Short left" is easier to stay for than a slow hello. Say the number or the risk out loud.

Ask a question the picture already answers

If the frame shows the boss, the line can be the question. The viewer stays to see if they were right.

Cut the greeting

A logo, a wave, or "hey guys" spends the only seconds that matter. Start after that.

Match the caption to the spoken line

If the voice says one thing and the text says another, people stall. Use the same words in both.

None of these need a new channel or a new niche. They need the moment to be first.
